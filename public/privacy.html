Privacy

Last updated November 4, 2026.

Liftoff is a daily estimation game at dailyliftoff.app. There are no accounts, and we never ask for your name, email or any other personal information.

What stays in your browser

  • A random player id, created the first time you play, so your guesses on one day can be told apart from someone else's. It is not tied to you or your device in any other way.
  • Your game progress: the run in progress, past results and streak, your scratch pad notes, and whether sound is on. These are kept in your browser's local storage and never sent to us, except as described below.

Clearing this site's data in your browser deletes all of it.

What we receive

  • Your guesses, sent with your random player id, so we can score them and show how other players did on the same question.
  • A few game events, also with the random id: when a launch is opened or finished, a result is copied to share, a past launch is replayed, or the calculator or scratch pad is used. We use these to see how the game is played and to improve it. Your scratch pad notes themselves are never sent.
  • Page visits, counted by Vercel Web Analytics: pages viewed, the referring site, country, and device type. It uses no cookies and doesn't identify you.

The game and its data are hosted by Vercel and Supabase. We don't sell this data or share it with anyone else.

Ads

Liftoff does not show ads today. If it does in future, they will come from Google AdSense, which may use cookies to show and measure ads. Visitors in the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland will be asked for consent first. You can read how Google uses information from sites that use its services and manage ad personalization in your Google ad settings. This page will be updated before any ads appear.

Tips

If you choose to tip, the payment happens on the tip service's own site, under its own privacy policy. We don't see your payment details.

Children

Liftoff is for a general audience and doesn't knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Questions

Open an issue at github.com/hjtan03/liftoff and we'll get back to you.
