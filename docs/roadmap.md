# Roadmap

Where Liftoff is going. These are outcomes, not plans: each says what the
player or the game gets and leaves how open. Nothing here is a commitment.
Items get reordered, split, merged, or dropped as we learn. Ideas for how,
including the first sketches, live in [the archive](archive/) and in pull
requests, not here. To change the roadmap, change this file in a pull
request and put the why in the commit.

Where things stand at launch (Saturday, October 24, 2026): question sets are
published through launch #069 (December 31) and passed a review for trivia
and copied questions; launches roll over at midnight Eastern; a reload keeps
the run and streaks count; the crowd number is real; the name is Liftoff for
now. Still open: sets for 2027, a bonus for hits the crowd missed, the
domain, and the stage-by-stage burn.

## Question supply

There are always more questions than days, and every one meets the bar:
answerable from sourced constants or numbers stated in the prompt, never
trivia.

## Known constants

Every constant a question leans on is sourced and well known enough that
reasoning gets you there.

## No repeats

No question repeats or nearly repeats an earlier one.

## A run that keeps

A reload does not lose the run, and there is a streak worth coming back
for.

## A real crowd

The crowd number is real, per question per day, and a hit the crowd missed
is worth more.

## A launch that tells the day

The rocket burns each fired stage in turn on the way up, so the climb shows
what happened.

## A name of its own

A name and a domain.
